About — Saleh Ghiyath

Practicalinnovation.

Auckland, New Zealand

I grew up watching people build remarkable things from almost nothing.

In Baghdad, under sanctions that severed supply chains and starved manufacturers of materials, ordinary people engineered extraordinary solutions — not out of genius, but necessity. That spirit of making it work regardless shaped how I approach every product I've touched since.

"Purpose means adding genuine value — to something I actually believe in. Not chasing trends." — Saleh Ghiyath

I moved to New Zealand and spent 20+ years engineering hardware products that shipped globally. Battery management systems for Yamaha. Marine electronics for NAVICO. A $2M storage range for Sistema Plastics — 21 tools, 7 patents, 12 months. A portable speaker chosen by Sony USA that sold 50,000 units in its first year.

I hold 7 patents and won the NZ Plastics Industry Gold Prize in 2004. I am not a designer who hands over renders and steps away. I'm an engineer who understands what happens when design meets the production floor — and I design so it survives that moment.
