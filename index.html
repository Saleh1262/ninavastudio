What we do

Three disciplines.
One studio.

We take hardware from sketch to production line — concept, CAD, engineering, factory. No handoffs to teams who weren't in the room.

01
Product Design

From first sketch to production-ready CAD. DFM is part of the thinking from day one — not a note left for manufacturing. SolidWorks, Creo, engineering drawings, photorealistic renders.

02
Mechanical Engineering

Enclosures, tolerances, materials, thermal and structural analysis. Products designed to survive the floor, not just impress in a prototype. IPX ratings, FEA, production trials.

03
Consulting

DFM audits, tooling strategy, factory oversight. Embedded engineering for hardware companies that need serious input — without the overhead of a full-time hire.
